Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Version 1

This policy explains what information Live Bet Tracker (the "App"), run by Live Bet LLC ("we", "us"), collects, how it's used and shared, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy and our Terms of Use.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a login ID, whether your email is confirmed, and that you confirmed you're 21+ and agreed to the Terms (with the date). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.
  • Your birth date is used only on your device to check your age when you sign up. It is not saved or sent to us.
  • Group activity: your display name, the groups you belong to, the bets you add or that sync from Polymarket (stakes, payouts, legs, results), daily pick'em picks, reactions, alerts, your watch list and settings such as "Hide my dollar amounts."
  • Polymarket key (optional): if you connect Polymarket US, your key is stored on your device, optionally locked with a PIN. If you turn on Background sync, an encrypted copy is stored in our database. It can only be unlocked by the group's sync job, not by other members or in the App.
  • Phone alerts (optional): if you turn them on, we store the push address your browser gives us, so we can send you alerts.
  • Basic technical data collected by our service providers (for example IP address, browser type and error logs) to run and protect the service.

We don't sell your information, we don't use advertising trackers, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

2. How we use it

  • To run the App: sign you in, show your group's bets and results, track live stats, send alerts you asked for, and settle bets.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and meet legal requirements.
  • To check your bets against Polymarket (read-only) if you turn on Background sync.

3. Who sees it

  • Your group: approved members see your display name, bets, results, picks and reactions. Dollar amounts are hidden if you turn that setting on. The Me tab, leak finder and your account totals are only shown to you.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (accounts, database and hosting), GitHub (runs the hourly sync job and stores backups for 30 days) and push services run by your phone or browser maker (such as Apple or Google) to deliver alerts. They process data for us under their own terms.
  • Data sources: your device requests scores, stats and prices directly from ESPN and Polymarket. If you connect Polymarket, your device and the sync job send signed, read-only requests to Polymarket using your key.
  • Legal reasons: we may share information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the App.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and group data are kept while your account is active. Backups are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Alerts are deleted after about 21 days. Settled bets saved on your device stay there until you sign out and clear them, or delete your account.

5. Your choices

  • Delete everything: Me → Account → Delete my account and data removes your account, bets, picks, reactions, alert settings and saved keys. Copies in backups are gone within 30 days.
  • Turn off Background sync or Phone alerts at any time. "Sign out & forget my Polymarket key" removes the key from your device.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it. A contact email for these requests is coming soon and will be listed in section 8. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have additional rights. We'll respond as the law requires.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to group data is limited to approved members by database rules. Saved Polymarket keys are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so use a unique password and a read-only Polymarket key.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make important changes, we'll show a notice in the App.

8. Contact

A contact email for privacy questions and requests is coming soon and will be listed here. Until then, you can delete your account and all your data at any time from Me → Account in the App.
