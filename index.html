High risk, high reward

The app compares DraftKings' odds with two independent estimates: ESPN's win-probability model and the Polymarket price for the same game. When those estimates say a team wins more often than the odds imply, that's a value spot. Small parlays are built from those spots. These are estimates, not sure things. Longshots lose most of the time, even when the price is good.
